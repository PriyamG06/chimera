# Release Notes — RN-2026-10

**Ship date:** 2026-10-01
**Team:** Discovery & Recommendations (PM: Leo Fontaine)
**Title:** Recommendation relevance optimization

## Summary

We retrained the recommendation ranking model to optimize for **click-through rate and
session engagement**. The new model weights historical engagement signals (clicks,
dwell time, completed purchases) much more heavily than the previous version.

## Results (first 2 weeks)

- Click-through rate on recommended storefronts: **+22%**
- Average session depth: **+15%**
- Buyer engagement time: **+18%**

## Team note

Great win for the discovery team. Buyers are clicking more and browsing deeper. This
validates the shift to engagement-weighted ranking.

## What we measured

Engagement metrics (CTR, dwell, session depth). 

> Note: we did **not** segment outcomes by creator cohort (new vs established) or look
> at transaction distribution across creators. Scope was engagement, not marketplace
> balance.
