# Abandoned Initiative: Creator Trust & Quality Scoring

**Date:** 2026-05-02
**Status:** SHELVED
**Owner (former):** Trust & Safety + Discovery

## What it was

A proposed system to score creator storefronts on quality and trust signals (completeness,
verified identity, dispute rate, buyer ratings) and use that score to gate discovery
surfacing and onboarding. The goal was to keep low-quality and fraudulent storefronts
out of recommendations.

## Why it was shelved

Deprioritized in favor of the Q3 acquisition push. Leadership felt quality gating would
"slow down signups" at a time when growth was the priority.

## Relevance note (for whoever revisits this)

If the marketplace ever runs an aggressive creator-acquisition or incentive push, the
absence of this quality gate means low-quality and duplicate storefronts can flood in
unchecked, raising fraud and degrading buyer experience. This capability does not
currently exist.
