## Auto-refund rules

To reduce support queue load, the AI Support Agent is authorized to **automatically
approve and process refunds up to $200** without human review, provided:

- The request is within 30 days of purchase.
- The buyer account is older than 7 days.
- No prior refund on the same order.

Refunds **above $200** require manual review by a support agent.

## Rationale

Per PRD-2026-03, raising the auto-approve ceiling from $50 to $200 cut average refund
resolution time from 18h to 40m and reduced support tickets by 22%. Buyers are happier;
support load is down.
