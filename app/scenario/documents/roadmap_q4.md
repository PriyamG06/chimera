# Q4 2026 Product Roadmap

**Owner:** CEO office
**Date:** 2026-09-30

## Theme: "Scale the top of funnel"

The company's #1 priority this quarter is **creator acquisition**. The board wants to
see creator-base growth ahead of the Series C conversation.

### Committed initiatives (ranked)

1. **Creator acquisition campaign** — paid + referral, target +40% signups.
2. **Creator incentive bonus** — one-time payout for new creators who publish 3+
   products in their first month. (Proposed; flag available: `creator_incentive_bonus`.)
3. **Enterprise creator onboarding** — white-glove for large creators (Sales-led).
4. **Checkout polish** — minor follow-ups to the RN-2026-09 redesign.

### Explicitly deprioritized

- Discovery / recommendation health review — "the rec engine was just optimized, no
  action needed."
- Refund / fraud workflow audit — "Risk is monitoring, not a product priority."
- Marketplace balance / long-tail creator success — "not a Q4 focus."

> This roadmap reflects leadership's belief that growth is the lever. It does not
> address transaction decline, discovery concentration, or the refund/fraud issue.
